# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

This privacy policy describes how this site and its associated application(s) — including the use of [rclone](https://rclone.org/) to access Google Drive — collect, use, and protect information.

## Overview

This site is operated by Willy A. Takang for personal, non-commercial use. It integrates with the Google Drive API (via rclone) to allow the site owner to sync and manage files in their own Google Drive account.

## Information We Access

When authorized via Google OAuth, this application accesses only the Google Drive data belonging to the account that grants permission. No data belonging to other users is accessed, and no data is collected from visitors to this site.

## How Information Is Used

Access to Google Drive data is used exclusively to read, write, and manage files within the authorized account, for the personal use of the site owner. Data is not used for any other purpose.

## Data Sharing

No data obtained through this integration is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the site owner's own device and are not transmitted to or stored on any third-party server.

## Data Retention

Authorization tokens remain valid until revoked by the account owner. Access can be revoked at any time through [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to wtakang@outlook.com.
